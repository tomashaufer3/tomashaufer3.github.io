Loyalty, Institutions, and Choicein the Historical Record

Quantitative economic history
Political economy · CERGE-EI

I turn historical records — service files, censuses, digitised text — into datasets large enough to test something, and use them to ask why people chose as they did. My master’s thesis follows roughly 133,000 Czechoslovak legionnaires who had already deserted one army to join another, and asks what made them stay in the second.

Current research

The Fateful Adventures of the Good Soldiers

Loyalty, desertion, and the Czechoslovak Legions, 1914–1920. Legionnaires had already deserted the Austro-Hungarian army before they joined; the same men then chose again, under different incentives. That second choice is what the design turns on.

  • Records
    ~133,000 individual legionnaire service files
  • Comparison
    ~186,000 fallen Habsburg soldiers
  • Geography
    350 districts 1910 census, with settlement crosswalks

Supervised by Sebastian Ottinger. Data construction is complete; the empirical specification is still being worked out.
