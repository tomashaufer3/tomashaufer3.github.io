Substack

Running since September 2025. The newsletter is where the working out happens; the research pages are where it ends up.

I write Diary of an Econ Student — mostly macroeconomics, condensed versions of my own papers, and whatever a book, an article, or a stubborn dataset has set off.

It started after the bachelor’s thesis, which I enjoyed writing far more than I expected to, and it has been a useful place to think in public ever since. It is written from where I actually stand: a master’s student working things out, not an authority handing down conclusions. If you want commentary from a Nobel laureate, this is the wrong newsletter.

The struggle is real… and so are the graphs.
