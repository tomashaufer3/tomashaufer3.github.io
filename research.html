Theses

  • The Fateful Adventures of the Good Soldiers: Loyalty, Desertion, and the Czechoslovak Legions

    Master’s thesis, CERGE-EI Supervisor: Sebastian Ottinger In progress

    Abstract

    What drives desertion in a volunteer army? The Czechoslovak Legions offer an unusual answer, because the men in them had already deserted once: legionnaires left the Austro-Hungarian army before joining, so the same individuals can be observed making two sequential loyalty decisions under different incentives. The study builds on roughly 133,000 individual service records covering 1914 to 1920, a comparison group of some 186,000 fallen Habsburg soldiers, and the 1910 census at the level of 350 administrative districts.

Term papers and projects

  • Written in Time: Unsupervised Discovery of Emotional and Stylistic Structure in the Project Gutenberg Corpus

    Machine Learning for Social Scientists, CERGE-EI 2026

    Abstract

    Whether machine learning and quantitative text analysis can recover emotional, stylistic, and thematic structure in literature over time. Starting from 62,181 English-language books in the Project Gutenberg corpus, the project builds a feature-level dataset of emotion scores, stylometric measures, and semantic fields, residualises those features on decade, country, and genre, and then applies clustering and network analysis to map similarity between books and authors. A final section studies changes around historical shocks: after 1914, expressed fear rises by roughly 0.16 of a standard deviation relative to baseline. The exercise is exploratory rather than causal — the point is to show how a large literary corpus becomes structured data.

  • What Drives Referee Performance?

    Statistics term project, CERGE-EI 2026

    Abstract

    The effect of referee coaches on referee performance, using an original panel dataset tracking performance, education, and career progression among Czech floorball referees in the Hradec Králové and Pardubice regions across the 2023/24 and 2024/25 seasons. Estimated with multiple fixed effects in Python.

Master’s student in economic research at CERGE-EI, working on the political economy of loyalty and desertion.
